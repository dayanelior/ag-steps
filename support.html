Steps missing or different?

Check that Steps records exist in Apple Health and that AG Steps has read access. Open AG Steps and pull down on Today to refresh. Live iPhone steps is the phone-only count; Health can combine data from iPhone and Apple Watch. These counts can differ and are never added together.

Apple Watch

Install AG Steps using the Watch app on your iPhone. Open it on your Watch and connect Apple Health. Your goal and theme sync from iPhone when connectivity permits. Each device reads its own Health store, so allow time for Apple Health synchronization. The Watch refreshes on opening, manually, and every minute while active.

Widgets look behind?

Open AG Steps to refresh its local summary. iOS controls widget scheduling, so updates may be delayed. After midnight, a new Health check is needed. Widgets do not measure steps themselves.

Goal Live Activity

In Settings, enable Goal milestones and choose an interval. For example, at 50% of an 8,000-step goal, the activity shows 4,000 steps remaining with a countdown to local midnight. It uses Apple Health totals, including Watch steps after synchronization.

A new activity starts when the app detects a milestone while open. Existing activities can update when iOS gives the app runtime; exact background timing is not guaranteed. iOS limits each activity to eight hours. Old information is labeled as last checked. Also check that Live Activities are allowed for AG Steps in iPhone Settings.

Haptics and appearance

Choose a theme and System, Light, or Dark appearance in Settings. Haptic feedback can be turned off. Live phone-step haptics are very soft, foreground-only, and limited to once every two seconds.

Permissions and reset

Manage read access in Apple Health and Motion & Fitness access in iPhone Settings. Reset local app data clears local iPhone preferences and the widget summary. It never deletes Apple Health records. See the privacy policy for details.
