Your privacy

Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026 · Developer: Elior Dayan

AG Steps is a free step counter for iPhone and Apple Watch. It has no accounts, ads, tracking, analytics SDKs, purchases, or subscriptions. The app does not upload your health or motion data to the developer.

Apple Health

With your permission, AG Steps reads only step counts to show daily progress, history, widgets, and goal Live Activities. The Watch app reads steps from its own Health store. AG Steps never adds, edits, or deletes Health records. You control read access in Apple’s Health privacy settings. Missing data may mean no readable records or that access is unavailable; the app cannot distinguish a denied read permission from missing data.

Optional live iPhone steps

If enabled, Motion & Fitness access lets the app read your iPhone pedometer while the app is active. This phone-only count stays in memory and is cleared when the app becomes inactive. It is not uploaded, written to Health, added to Health totals, or shared with widgets. Turn off Live iPhone steps to stop, or manage permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness.

Local storage and Apple Watch

Your goal and preferences are stored locally. Recent history is held in memory. A minimal summary for widgets contains today’s available steps, goal, theme, day boundaries, and last-check time. This protected local cache is excluded from app-controlled backup. Goal and theme preferences are transferred to your paired Watch using Apple’s WatchConnectivity. No step records are transferred by that preference channel.

Widgets and Live Activities

Widgets and optional Live Activities can display step data on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and compatible Apple system surfaces. Live Activities use a minimal count, goal, theme, day boundaries, and last-check date. Enable them only if you are comfortable with that visibility. Widget visibility and Live Activities have separate settings. System-rendered content may remain until the system refreshes it or you dismiss it.

Your choices and deletion

Turn off widget data to hide the shared summary. Turn off Goal milestones to end the Live Activity. Reset local app data resets the iPhone’s preferences and widget cache without deleting Apple Health records or revoking Health access. Remove the Watch app to remove its local preferences. You can separately revoke Health and Motion access using Apple’s settings. The developer holds no copy of your Health records to retrieve or delete.

Apple services

Apple controls Health synchronization, App Store and TestFlight services, and operating-system diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy. AG Steps does not operate those services. It uses no developer-operated server for step tracking.

Support communications and this website

If you choose to email support, your email address and the information you send are received through the developer’s email provider and used to answer your request. Please do not send Health records or other sensitive information. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact the address below. This website uses no developer-added analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, or contact forms. The hosting provider may process standard connection information, such as IP address and request details, to deliver and secure the website.
